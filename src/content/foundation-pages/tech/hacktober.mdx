Get ready for Hacktober 2026! Throughout October (October 1–31), join the Interledger Foundation in strengthening our open-source ecosystem. We are excited to collaborate with builders, developers, and enthusiasts around the globe!

## About Hacktober

[Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/) is an annual event that promotes open-source contributions throughout October. It aims to support and give back to open-source projects that underpin today’s tech infrastructure. Over the past decade, thousands of coders and non-coders have joined to support their favorite projects, improve their skills, and connect with like-minded open-source enthusiasts. We express our support for this initiative by running a Hacktober program of activities to engage the open source community.

## **Prizes & Recognition**

We appreciate every contribution that improves the Interledger ecosystem! As a token of gratitude, there are 2 exclusive prizes for every participant, plus a special prize for the top contributor!

</Paragraph>

<CardGrid ariaLabel="prizes" variant="Info" columns="Three">
<InfoCard heading="Digital Badge">

Earn a limited-edition Interledger digital badge for any valid contribution made in either category during the event. There are no complex leaderboards, tiers, or points to track.

</InfoCard>
<InfoCard heading="Swag Pack">

The top contributor of the month will receive an exclusive Interledger physical swag pack! The top contributor will be selected based on the number of qualifying PRs and Forms submissions (see section _Contribution Guidelines and AI Policy_ above)

</InfoCard>
